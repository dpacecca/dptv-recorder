All three stream-copy only - no re-encoding, no quality loss, no extra CPU/GPU cost. The difference is just the container and when it's finalized: Matroska (.mkv) needs a proper trailer/seek-index written at the end to be well-formed, so a still-recording .mkv usually won't play (or seek) reliably in Plex - raw .ts has no such requirement and is readable while it's still growing. "Recommended" records as .ts (so you can watch it live/in progress) and automatically remuxes to a properly finished .mkv once it's done - best of both.
Recordings are written inside the container at /recordings, which maps to the RECORDINGS_DIR folder you set in .env / docker-compose.yml on the host.
Notifications look like: Scheduled recording of "Program Name" has started/completed/failed. Create an application in Gotify to get a token - see Apps in your Gotify instance.
